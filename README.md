# Fibroid Patient Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive Healthcare Analytics Dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze fibroid patient demographics, symptom patterns, disease severity, treatment distribution, and clinical outcomes. The dashboard aims to support data-driven healthcare decision-making by identifying key patient trends, treatment effectiveness, and potential risk factors associated with fibroid conditions.

---

## Business Problem

Fibroids are among the most common gynecological conditions affecting women of reproductive age. Healthcare providers require actionable insights to better understand patient demographics, symptom burden, treatment outcomes, and recurrence patterns.

This dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

* Which age groups are most affected by fibroids?
* What symptoms drive the highest healthcare utilization?
* How severe are fibroid cases across different age groups?
* Which treatment options produce the best clinical outcomes?
* What patient characteristics may influence fibroid prevalence?

---

## Objectives

* Analyze patient demographics and clinical characteristics.
* Identify the most affected age groups.

## Key Insights

### 1. Women aged 36–45 are the most affected group

The highest concentration of fibroid cases was observed among patients aged 36–45 years.

### 2. High BMI prevalence among patients

Approximately 77.5% of patients were classified as overweight or obese, indicating a potential association between elevated BMI and fibroid occurrence.

### 3. Large fibroids dominate patient cases

Large fibroids accounted for the highest number of cases, suggesting delayed diagnosis or treatment-seeking behavior.

### 4. Pelvic pain and gastrointestinal-related symptoms generate the highest hospital visits

Symptoms such as pelvic pain, constipation, and bloating contributed significantly to healthcare utilization.

### 5. Surgical treatment achieved the strongest clinical outcomes

Surgery recorded the highest successful response rate compared to other treatment approaches.

## Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen early screening programs

Target women within the 36–45 age group for routine screening and awareness initiatives.

### 2. Promote healthy lifestyle interventions

Implement programs focused on weight management and overall reproductive health.

### 3. Improve early detection strategies

Encourage earlier diagnosis to reduce the prevalence of large fibroid cases.

### 4. Focus on symptom management

Develop targeted care plans for patients experiencing high-burden symptoms such as pelvic pain and constipation.

### 5. Monitor treatment effectiveness continuously

Use outcome-based analysis to optimize treatment pathways and improve patient care.
